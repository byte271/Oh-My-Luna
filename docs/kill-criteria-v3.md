# Kill criteria V3

Status: pre-registered before Gate H  
Supersedes: `kill-criteria.md` for the reset research direction

## Gate M

Do not start Gate H if treatment-specific inputs can expose other levels,
hidden material reaches the adapter or scorer, freeze mutation is not rejected,
receipt statuses conflate configured-verifier success with claim evidence, or
reviewers cannot apply the boundary rubric consistently. Collapse L3 and L4
into one “decisive diagnostic fact” level if independent reviewers cannot
reliably distinguish observation from diagnosis. Prefer a valid shorter ladder.

## Gate H

- Do not approximate a category whose oracle arm has no materially useful
  paired end-to-end lift over native, equal-token, and equal-cost controls.
- Reject a purported lift if payload specificity or patch similarity indicates
  solution leakage, or if a less specific rewrite removes the effect.
- Do not call the highest arm the bottleneck; identify the earliest level
  meeting the frozen minimum-sufficient rule.
- If correct context and diagnosis do not materially help, classify the gap as
  substantially model-bound and narrow to reliability or explicit hybrid use.
- If the representative slice regresses, do not hide it behind high-gap gains.

## Gate A

- Kill a learned component if it does not beat the strongest deterministic or
  training-free baseline on repository-disjoint end-to-end repair.
- Kill the practical approximation if equal-total-cost Luna is non-inferior,
  gains disappear on held-out repositories, or assisted losses exceed 25% of
  assisted wins.
- Kill or make hybrid operation explicit if total cost exceeds 50% of Sol while
  success remains materially below 95% of Sol.
- A gain in retrieval, calibration, or oracle-fidelity metrics without a held-
  out repair gain is a failed product result.
- Do not proceed to a second specialist unless the first passes Gate A.

## Release claim

No claim ships from Gate M or a 12–20-task pilot. A release claim requires a
pre-registered repository-disjoint held-out sample sized from task-level
variance and the minimum meaningful effect, exact snapshots and settings,
separate representative/high-gap reporting, total-system cost, and all
redistributable attempts and sanitized traces.
